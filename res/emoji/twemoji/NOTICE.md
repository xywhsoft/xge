# Twemoji graphics subset

Source: https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji

The bundled subset is used by the XGE built-in SVG Emoji pack. The graphics
license is preserved in `LICENSE-GRAPHICS`.

Included sequences cover the Emoji rendering example and regression tests:
grinning face, face with tears of joy, heart, thumbs up, medium-skin-tone
thumbs up, rocket, fire, check mark button, warning, party popper, family, and
the China flag.
