# XGE contextual whole-word fixtures

Generated by `test/generate_word_context_fixture.py` from project-owned outlines,
cmap and metrics. No external font is copied. Timestamps are fixed; FontTools
is only required to regenerate the two committed TTFs.

Both fonts use glyph IDs 2=f, 3=i, 4=x, 8=Greek alpha, 9=acute,
10=e and 11=eacute. Private-use aliases U+E600+glyphID address the literal glyphs.
The primary omits i, Greek alpha, e and acute from its normal cmap, but has
eacute so HarfBuzz can cover the complete NFC-normalized e+acute grapheme.

The primary's f/x advance is 200 font units and its short outlines are visibly
different. The full font has f=400, i=300 and x=500 units with taller outlines.
Units per em is 1000; ascent/descent are 800/-200. There are no GSUB lookups.
A 40px split ffi must therefore choose full fonts and end at 44px, regardless
of the style/font boundaries. The primary-only first f ends at 8px rather than
the required 16px. This makes wrong font choice observable in both geometry
and a complete RGBA comparison, without relying on OS fonts or rerendering
the contextual text as its own reference.

Standalone acute has zero advance and an outward-rounded 100-unit raster ink
bound. A covering NFC grapheme does not imply its e/acute slices can be shaped
independently in that font. Public regressions check both facts.
